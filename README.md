# Coop Knockout 🐔🥊

A mascot boxing game for the browser. Regular people in chicken and barnyard animal costumes slug it out in the ring. Knock one out and their costume head **pops up on a spring**, revealing the sweaty human inside.

## Barnyard Ladder
Pick a fighter and climb six rungs: Round 1, Round 2, the Quarterfinal and the Semifinal, then a **Challenger** and the **Championship**. The computer gets tougher at every rung.
- The **Challenger** is a mystery fighter you haven't unlocked yet. Beat them to unlock them.
- The **Championship** is against the Golden Goose. Beat the boss to unlock it.
- Lose a fight and you can retry it.
- Unlocks are saved in your browser. It takes four ladder wins to unlock everyone.

**To play,** open `index.html` in any modern browser. There's nothing to install or build.

## Modes
- **Barnyard Ladder:** a 1-player climb that unlocks new fighters (see above)
- **Quick Match vs CPU:** Easy, Normal or Hard
- **2 Players** on the same keyboard
- **Phones and tablets:** on-screen touch buttons for Player 1.
  - **Held sideways:** the whole ring fills the screen, and the buttons sit in the side margins so they never cover the fight.
  - **Held upright:** the camera zooms in and follows the fighters, with a full-size control pad below.
  - Tap ❚❚ to pause or quit.

## Controls
| Action  | Player 1 | Player 2 |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Move    | A / D    | ← / →    |
| Block   | S        | ↓        |
| Dodge   | W        | ↑        |
| Jab     | F        | J        |
| Hook    | G        | K        |
| Special | H        | L        |

In solo play either key set works, and Space also dodges. P / Esc pauses, M mutes.

## Fight night
- **Ring entrances:** before round 1, each fighter walks out under a spotlight while the announcer introduces them. Expect a fanfare, a nickname ("The Hog of War"), a fight record and who's inside the suit, followed by a crowd-pleasing pose. Then the referee calls them together for the rules. Tap or press Enter to skip.
- **The referee** is a person in a zebra suit. He comes over to count knockdowns, chopping his arm on each count.
- **The winner ceremony:** when the match is over, the ref walks to the middle of the ring and lifts the winner's glove high. Bells ring and confetti flies, while the loser slumps (or is still flat out with their head on its spring).

## Rules
- Best of 3 rounds, 60 seconds each.
- **Jab** is fast with low damage. **Hook** is slow and hits hard. Hitting someone during their wind-up is a **COUNTER** for bonus damage.
- **Block** cuts damage but drains stamina. If your stamina runs out, your guard breaks.
- **Stamina** is spent on punches and dodges. When you're tired, you move and punch slower and weaker.
- Big hits make your costume head wobble on its spring.
- **Special meter** fills as you land and take hits. When it's full, unleash your mascot's special, a dashing haymaker.
- When health hits 0 your head **pops off** (BOING!). Mash punch keys to shove it back on before the count of 10. Each knockdown makes getting up harder, and three knockdowns in a round is a **T.K.O.**
- If time runs out, the round goes to whoever has more health, with a penalty for each knockdown.
